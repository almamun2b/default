# Word lists in src/data/

Used to name emails (see README "How names are chosen"). The big lists are generated by `scripts/build-word-lists.ts` from the downloads below and our own stored data; the small ones are hand-made. The downloads themselves are not kept in the repository. Re-running the script never removes an entry from a hand-made list.

| File | What it is | Made from |
|---|---|---|
| `given-names.txt` | first names | hand-made list + US Social Security Administration baby names (names in the yearly top 1000 for 5+ years, via github.com/hadley/data-baby-names, 2026-11-11). Public domain (US government data). Common English words and places are kept out unless they are very common names |
| `surnames.txt` | surnames in rank order | US Census Bureau 2010 surnames, `www2.census.gov/topics/genealogy/2010surnames/names.zip` (2026-11-11), the 50,000 most common. Public domain |
| `places.txt` | suburb, locality and town words (AU and NZ) | ABS Australian Statistical Geography Standard, Suburbs and Localities 2021 (`SAL_2021_AUST.xlsx`) and the LINZ NZ Gazetteer (`gazetteer.linz.govt.nz/gaz.csv`), both 2026-11-11. See attribution below |
| `free-mail-domains.txt` | free and ISP mail providers | hand-made list + `Kikobeats/free-email-domains` `domains.json` (2026-11-11). MIT |
| `role-words.txt` | words that make a mailbox a role or office, not a person (senator, workshop, kundendienst …) | hand-made, plus local parts found on 15+ domains in our stored emails, reviewed by eye |
| `place-words.txt` | places that are also given names (sydney, preston) | hand-made |
| `name-words.txt` | vocabulary and weights for splitting `anytimefitness` into words | words from our scraped business names and input name columns, the lists above, and the English dictionary (SCOWL, `/usr/share/dict/american-english` and `british-english`, 4 to 14 lowercase letters) |

## Refreshing

```bash
# download into fresh folders: ssa/baby-names.csv, census/x/Names_2010Census.csv (unzipped), abs/SAL_2021_AUST.xlsx, linz/gaz.csv, free/domains.json
npx tsx scripts/build-word-lists.ts --from /path/to/downloads
npx tsx scripts/build-word-lists.ts --role-candidates   # local parts on 15+ domains, to review and add to role-words.txt by hand
```

Then run `npx tsx scripts/recheck-cache.ts` and review the changes.
